Inside the 9 chapters

  1. Fundamental Analysis
    Read the health of a company before buying its stock: PE ratio, ROE, revenue growth, and where to find the data for NSE stocks.
  2. Technical Analysis
    What TA is, how it works, FA vs TA, types of analysis — and the honest limitations every beginner must know.
  3. How to Read a Stock Chart
    Candlestick anatomy, timeframes, trend direction, support & resistance, volume, VWAP & EMA, entry signals.
  4. 5 Candlestick Patterns That Actually Work
    Doji, Hammer, Engulfing, Morning Star, Shooting Star — with real NSE chart examples and entry/SL rules.
  5. 11 Technical Indicators, Explained Simply
    VWAP, EMA, MACD, RSI, Volume Surge, ADX, Supertrend, Bollinger Bands, ATR, OBV, Pivot Points + the 5-Gate Checklist.

  8. How to Place a Trade
    Market vs limit orders, an execution checklist, intraday (MIS) vs swing (CNC), common execution mistakes.
  9. Trading Psychology
    FOMO, fear, greed, overconfidence — and the daily mental routine that separates consistent traders from emotional ones.
