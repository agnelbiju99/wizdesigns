What exactly do I get?

How do I use it?

Open the link in Chrome or Safari, then bookmark it or tap 'Add to Home screen' so it opens like an app. No app to install and no account.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It's made for phones and also works on tablets and laptops.

Is it in Malayalam?

Yes. Tap മല at the top to switch the whole app to Malayalam, and EN to switch back.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You add your loans, cards and dues yourself. That also means nobody can see your money details: everything stays on your own phone.

Will it remind me before due dates?

It doesn't send notifications. Open it once a week: the dashboard and calendar show what's due, what's paid and what's overdue.

Can I use it on my phone and laptop?

Each device keeps its own data and they don't sync. Download a backup on one device and restore it on the other.

Is this financial advice?

No. It's a tool to track your own payments. Calculations are estimates, so always check exact amounts with your bank or lender.
